# Protocol steps
1. Input Phase:
    Referee sends random bits x and y to Alice and Bob respectively.

2. Preparation Phase:
    Alice and Bob share a Bell pair

3. Measurement Phase:
    - Alice measures her qubit in basis x.Pi/2 (0 or 90)
    - Bob measures his qubit in basis -y.Pi/2 (-45 or 45)

4. Output Phase:
    Alice and Bob outputs measurement results a and b respectively.

5. Winning criteria:
    Alice and Bob wins iff (a xor b) = (x xor y)
